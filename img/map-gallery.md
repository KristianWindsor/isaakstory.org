*The Duchy of Warsaw, 1809–1813.*

Napoleon’s Polish client state reached its peak after the Treaty of Schönbrunn added Kraków and former Austrian lands in October 1809. It was a short-lived sovereignty; by February 1813, Russian forces had occupied Warsaw, and the Duchy dissolved into a new European order two years later.
