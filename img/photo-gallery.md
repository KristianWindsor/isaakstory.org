![The Eureka Hardware Store, 1892](/img/photos/1892-eureka-hardware-store.jpg)

*The Hardware Store, Eureka, South Dakota, 1892.*

The oldest building in Eureka, it was first run by David Isaak and Charles Mewing, then passed to David's sons Albert and Ed. Albert's daughter, Edith Allegra, grew up within these walls.

Source: [Eureka Pioneer Museum Collection](https://digitalcollections.northern.edu/digital/collection/Eureka/id/70/rec/3)
