![The Eureka Hardware Store, 1892](/img/photos/1892-eureka-hardware-store.jpg)

*The Hardware Store, Eureka, South Dakota, 1892.*

The oldest building in Eureka, it was first run by David Isaak and Charles Mewing, then passed to David's sons Albert and Ed. Albert's daughter, Edith Allegra, grew up within these walls.

Source: [Eureka Pioneer Museum Collection](https://digitalcollections.northern.edu/digital/collection/Eureka/id/70/rec/3)

![The Eureka City Band, 1922](/img/photos/1922-eureka-band.jpg)

*The Eureka City Band, Eureka, South Dakota, 1922.*

Albert and his cousins Otto, Walther, Edwin, and Hank all played in the Eureka City Band.

Back Row: Ted Keim, Otto Isaak, George Kelle, F. Schenzenbach, Reinhardt Harr, John P. Kiesz, Walther Isaak. Middle Row: Otellia Kiesz, Esther Homeyer, Lillian Kiesz, E.L. Fischer, Henry Isaak, Rose Liefeld, Maude Gerdes, Ella Schamber, Alice Homeyer. Front Row: Albert Isaak, Lukas Balliet, Herbert Liedle, Jake Kiesz, Emil Schenkenberger, Ervin Siegele, Paul Liefeld, Edwin Isaak, Goat Stuart, -Leader.

Source: [Eureka Pioneer Museum Collection](https://digitalcollections.northern.edu/digital/collection/Eureka/id/771/rec/1)
