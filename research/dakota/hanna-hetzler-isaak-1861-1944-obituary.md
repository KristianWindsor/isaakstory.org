# Eureka Woman Dies

Eureka, S. D.—Special: Funeral services were held for Mrs. Hanna Hetzler Isaak at the Lutheran church with interment in the Lutheran cemetery. Daughter of the late Rev. and Mrs. Henry Hetzler, she was born in LaGrange, Ill. February 24, 1861. On August 30, 1883, she married Davids Isaak at Scotland, S. D., where they made their home for three years, after which they moved to the northern part of the state.
