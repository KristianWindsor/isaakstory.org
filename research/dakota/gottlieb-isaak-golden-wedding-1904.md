# Golden Wedding of Gottlieb Isaak and Wife

## Local Report

- Mr. and Mrs. Jakob Klein of Artas had been in Eureka since Saturday visiting. They also attended the golden wedding celebration of Mr. and Mrs. Gottlieb Isaak here on Saturday evening.

- Rev. H. Reinhardt took up a collection at the jubilee celebration in the city hall on Saturday evening as a Christmas gift for children in the orphanage at Sioux Falls, S. D. He collected a total of $25, which he sent there on Monday.

- Pastor Otto Bruntsch of Jewell, N. D., was in Eureka on business Monday. Rev. Bruntsch is a son-in-law of Mr. and Mrs. Gottlieb Isaak, who celebrated their golden wedding here Saturday evening. Mr. Bruntsch reported that he and his wife would very much have liked to attend the golden jubilee celebration, but they could not get away because of a death in the pastor's congregation there at the time.

## The First Golden Wedding in Eureka

**Mr. and Mrs. Gottlieb Isaak celebrated their golden wedding on Saturday evening in the family circle and in the presence of many relatives, acquaintances, and friends.**

Mr. and Mrs. Gottlieb Isaak celebrated their golden wedding on Saturday evening. In honor of the elderly bride and groom, a worship service was held at the local Lutheran Zion Church at 7:30 Saturday evening, on which occasion the church was filled with worshipers to the last seat. The bride wore a golden wreath and the groom a golden boutonniere, which their youngest son, Imanuel of Sheboygan, Wisconsin, had sent them.

After the service, Pastor H. Reinhardt, on behalf of the Zion congregation, presented the bridal couple with a very fine and magnificent Bible as a remembrance of the celebration and invited everyone present to go directly from the church to the city hall, where a spread of delicacies awaited the guests. About 200 guests took part in this banquet.

The Eureka band and the Neuherz Orchestra, composed of young boys, had been engaged for this celebration and alternated in playing their finest and best-rehearsed pieces. After supper came a congenial, genuinely German wedding celebration, at which everyone enjoyed themselves splendidly with German conversation, games, and dancing. On this occasion, the bridal couple received numerous congratulations and various valuable gifts. Especially noteworthy was the best and finest gift, presented to the elderly couple by their many relatives and friends in cash. It reached the handsome sum of $125.00.

Mr. and Mrs. Isaak now have nine living children, 40 grandchildren, and two great-grandchildren. Mr. Gottlieb Isaak is 70 years old and his wife is 67.

May the elderly couple live through many more years crowned with happiness and success and together celebrate their diamond wedding in good spirits and health, is the wish of the *Post*.

## Acknowledgment

To all dear friends and acquaintances who, by attending the celebration of our golden wedding in such numbers and through their generous gifts, brought us an unexpected and exceedingly great joy, we hereby express our most heartfelt thanks.

In particular, we also thank the Eureka band for its kind participation, as well as all those who helped make the celebration more beautiful in any way.

Gottlieb Isaak and wife.
