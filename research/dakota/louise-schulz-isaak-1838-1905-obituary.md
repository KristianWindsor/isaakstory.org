# Died.

On Monday, May 1, at 8 o'clock in the morning, the wife of Gottlieb Isaak died [illegible]. She was born in the year 1838, on May 12, in Kulm, South Russia, and married Gottlieb Isaak in the year 1855. They then emigrated to America in the year 1878, and lived for 8 years in Parkston, S. D., and 6 years in Mercer Co., N. D., after which they settled in Eureka, and lived here 13 years without interruption.

The funeral took place on Wednesday, May 3, at 2 o'clock in the afternoon. Pastor H. Reinhardt of the Ev. Lutheran congregation delivered the funeral sermon. She reached the age of 67 years, and leaves behind 9 married children, a number of grandchildren, and 2 great-grandchildren, together with her mourning husband. Peace to her ashes!

Beloved ones, when my spirit has departed,  
Weep no tears for me.  
For where I dwell, there is peace,  
There an eternal day shines upon me.  
Where all earthly sorrow has vanished,  
Your image shall not fade from my sight,  
And relief for your wounds,  
For your pain, I will implore:  
When Peace nightly wafts its wings of blessing.  
Over the realm of the world,  
Then think no more upon my grave,  
For from the stars I greet you.
