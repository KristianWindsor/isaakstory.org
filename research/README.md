# Research & Historical Context

This directory houses the primary and secondary sources used to build the world of this story.

These documents preserve the real-world history of the Isaak ancestors, detailing what daily life, work, faith, and migration actually looked like for German Lutheran colonists in Poland, Bessarabia, and the American Dakotas. Everything is formatted in Markdown to serve a dual purpose: it is easily readable for humans browsing the repository, and it provides clean, factual grounding for AI models assisting with the drafting process.

## What You'll Find Here

Sources are organized by place and topic, spanning the regions and eras the family lived through. They include translated regional histories, genealogical records, obituaries, personal recollections, and general cultural context — drawn from books, archival documents, websites, and video transcriptions.

The folders follow the family's migration — `poland/` up to about 1815, `bessarabia/` from 1815 to 1878, and `dakota/` from 1878 onward — each holding sources from the years the family actually lived there. `full-texts/` keeps the complete books and transcripts that span more than one era.

Every file opens with a short block recording where it came from and how it was transcribed or translated, so any detail can be traced back to its source; the conventions behind that live in [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md).

Whether you're an AI parsing context for a scene or a human history buff browsing the repo, feel free to explore.

## A Note on Sources & Copyright

This is a research collection, not a republication. While some items (like Wikipedia articles) are openly licensed, others are translations or books that remain under their authors' copyright and are kept here strictly as reference material for the novel. Please do not treat their presence here as permission to reuse them — always follow the original source's terms, which are recorded in each file's front matter.
