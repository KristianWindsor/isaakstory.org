# Research & Historical Context

This directory houses the primary and secondary sources used to build the world of this story. 

These documents preserve the real-world history of my ancestors, detailing what daily life, work, faith, and migration actually looked like for Bessarabian German colonists. Everything is formatted in Markdown to serve a dual purpose: it is easily readable for humans browsing the repository, and it provides clean, factual grounding for the AI models assisting with the drafting process.

## What You'll Find Here

Sources are organized by place and topic, spanning the regions and eras the family
lived through. They include translated regional histories, genealogical records,
obituaries, personal recollections, and general cultural context — drawn from
Wikipedia (including translated German, Polish, and Russian pages), books, archival
documents, websites, and video transcriptions.

Whether you're an AI parsing context for a scene or a human history buff browsing the
repo, feel free to explore.

## A Note on Sources & Copyright

This is a research collection, not a republication.
